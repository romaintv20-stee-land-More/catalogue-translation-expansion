## Marathi Minecraft 26.3 localization

The embedded Marathi (`mr_in`) Minecraft language file in the dedicated 26.3 Fabric and NeoForge candidate JARs was copied from the user's own [Controlling Language Expansion](https://github.com/romaintv20-stee-land-More/controlling-language-expansion) translation, which was derived from the user's Beyond & More 26.1.2 Marathi localization. Its later new/changed entries contain machine-assisted translation and require native-language review. The original Catalogue mod itself is not bundled; users must install its corresponding upstream release.
